# Before and after

These examples are original and illustrative. Use them to recognize patterns, not as reusable boilerplate.

## Project update

Before:

> We are excited to share that we have made significant progress across several important areas, and we are now entering the next phase of our journey.

After:

> The settings screen is ready for review. The remaining work is keyboard navigation and the mobile empty state.

Why: The revision says what changed and what remains.

## Scope discussion

Before:

> This isn't about moving faster, it's about setting ourselves up for long-term success through better alignment.

After:

> We need the requirements and an agreed scope before committing to Monday.

Why: The revision names the missing decision.

## Security product UI

Before:

> ACTIONABLE INTELLIGENCE
>
> Unlock smarter decisions across your ecosystem.
>
> Button: Explore now

After:

> Unassigned assets
>
> 12 assets have no owner.
>
> Button: Review assets

Why: The heading, detail, and action refer to the same task.

## Empty state

Before:

> NOTHING HERE YET
>
> Your journey begins here. Add context to unlock the power of your workspace.
>
> Button: Get started

After:

> No context added
>
> Add context to connect findings to the people and systems behind them.
>
> Button: Add context

Why: The reader can see what is missing and what action is available.

## Error

Before:

> Oops! Something went wrong. Don't worry, we've got you covered.

After:

> We couldn't save your changes. Try again.

Why: The text identifies the failure and offers a next step without invented reassurance.

## Documentation

Before:

> In this section, we'll dive into the powerful capabilities of the configuration system.

After:

> Set `UNIQUE_TAG` to the environment tag you want to deploy.

Why: Documentation should provide an instruction the developer can follow.
